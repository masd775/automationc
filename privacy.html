Privacy Policy

AutomationC · Effective November 19, 2026

AutomationC is a private tool that uploads videos to its owner's YouTube channel. This policy explains what information it accesses through Google and what it does with it.

Information accessed

  • Upload videos (youtube.upload): to upload videos to the connected channel.
  • View your YouTube account (youtube.readonly): to read the channel's name, to confirm the correct channel is connected.

AutomationC does not access any other Google data.

How it is used

This access is used only for the two purposes above. Google data is not used for advertising, not sold, and not used to train AI models.

Storage and sharing

The Google sign-in token is stored on the owner's own computer and is sent only to Google. AutomationC runs no servers and does not share Google user data with anyone.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time on the Google Account permissions page. Deleting the token file removes the stored sign-in.

YouTube API Services

AutomationC uses YouTube API Services. See the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy. AutomationC's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Contact

Changes to this policy will be posted on this page. For questions, use the support email address shown on the Google sign-in screen for AutomationC.
